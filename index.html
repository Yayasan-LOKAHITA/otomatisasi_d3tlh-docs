Welcome to Otomatisasi D3TLH

Overview

Otomatisasi D3TLH is a QGIS plugin developed to streamline and automate workflows for D3TLH (Environmental Carrying Capacity and Environmental Carrying Capability) assessments. The plugin integrates demographic, ecological, environmental, and spatial analysis models into a unified processing framework, reducing manual data preparation and calculation efforts. It provides tools for generating standardized outputs that support environmental planning, regional development assessment, and decision-making processes.

Author

The plugin was developed by the Directorate for environmental impact prevention and regional sectoral policy, Ministry of Environment of the Republic of Indonesia (KLH/BPLH), with support from Yayasan Lokus Bijak Hijau Lestari (LOKAHITA).
