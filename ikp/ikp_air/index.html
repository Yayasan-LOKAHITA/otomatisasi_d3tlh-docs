Biodiversity Utilization Capacity Index (IKP Kehati)

Overview

This module calculates the Biodiversity Utilization Capacity Index (IKP Kehati) by evaluating the balance between water availability and water demand within each analysis grid.

The methodology integrates river basin resources, water quality conditions, land cover characteristics, and population pressure to estimate the capacity of ecosystems to support biodiversity and human activities sustainably.

The resulting index can be used to identify areas experiencing high environmental pressure and support environmental carrying capacity assessments, spatial planning, and D3TLH workflows.

Purpose

To assess the utilization capacity of biodiversity resources through a water-based carrying capacity approach by comparing:

  • Available usable water resources
  • Water demand from land cover activities
  • Water demand from population

The analysis produces:

  • Usable Water Availability
  • Water Demand
  • Biodiversity Utilization Capacity Index (IKP)
  • IKP Classification
  • IKP Score

Required Inputs

1. Grid Layer

Grid layer generated using the Utility module.

Required field:

  • ID

2. Water Supply Providers Layer (PYA)

Vector dataset representing water source areas.

3. River Basin Layer (WS)

Vector river basin or watershed boundaries used to aggregate water availability.

4. Land Cover Layer (PL)

Land cover dataset used to estimate water demand.

Required field:

  • PL

5. Population Data

Population dataset containing population values for each administrative unit or grid.

6. Pollution Index Table

Table containing water quality information used to estimate polluted and usable water resources.

Output

The algorithm produces an IKP Kehati Grid Layer.

Each grid cell contains:

Field Description
ID Grid identifier
AIR_LAYAK Usable water availability
IKP Biodiversity Utilization Capacity Index
KELAS_IKP IKP classification
SKOR_IKP IKP score (1–5)

Methodology

The index is calculated by comparing total water demand against usable water availability.

Water Availability Processing

Water supply areas are intersected with the grid layer to calculate:

  • Water area within each grid
  • Water proportion within each grid

The results are aggregated by river basin (WS) to estimate:

AIR_WS

where:

  • AIR_WS = Available water resources within each river basin

Water Quality Processing

Pollution Index data are joined to the grid layer.

The module calculates:

AIR_CEMAR

and:

AIR_LAYAK

where:

  • AIR_CEMAR = Polluted water volume
  • AIR_LAYAK = Usable water availability after considering pollution

Water Demand from Land Cover

Water demand is estimated based on land cover characteristics:

W_PL

where:

  • W_PL = Water demand from land cover activities

Water Demand from Population

Population-based water demand is calculated as:

BA_POP

where:

  • BA_POP = Water demand generated by population

Total Water Demand

Total water demand is calculated as:

BA_TOTAL = W_PL + BA_POP

IKP Calculation

The Biodiversity Utilization Capacity Index is calculated as:

IKP = BA_TOTAL / AIR_LAYAK

where:

  • BA_TOTAL = Total water demand
  • AIR_LAYAK = Usable water availability

IKP Classification

IKP values are classified into five categories:

Class Description
1 Very Low
2 Low
3 Moderate
4 High
5 Very High

The classification results are stored in:

KELAS_IKP

and assigned a score in:

SKOR_IKP

Processing Workflow

  1. Harmonize the coordinate reference system (CRS) of all input datasets.
  2. Repair invalid geometries and create spatial indexes.
  3. Load the Grid, PYA, WS, Land Cover, Population, and Pollution Index datasets.
  4. Intersect the Grid layer with Water Supply Areas (PYA).
  5. Calculate water area and water proportion for each grid.

  6. Aggregate water resources by river basin (WS) to obtain:

  7. AIR_WS

  8. Join Pollution Index data to the grid.

  9. Calculate:

  10. AIR_CEMAR

  11. AIR_LAYAK

  12. Calculate land-cover-based water demand:

  13. W_PL

  14. Calculate population-based water demand:

    • BA_POP

    • Calculate:

    • BA_TOTAL

    • Calculate:

    • IKP

    • Classify IKP values into:

    • Very High

    • High
    • Moderate
    • Low
    • Very Low

    • Assign:

    • KELAS_IKP

    • SKOR_IKP
    • Generate the final IKP Kehati grid layer.

Output Interpretation

High IKP Values

Indicate:

  • Water demand exceeds available resources
  • Higher environmental pressure
  • Greater utilization intensity
  • Potential biodiversity degradation risk

Low IKP Values

Indicate:

  • Water availability exceeds demand
  • Lower environmental pressure
  • Better ecosystem support capacity
  • Greater sustainability potential

High SKOR_IKP

Indicate areas requiring attention for biodiversity and water resource management.

Low SKOR_IKP

Indicate areas with relatively balanced water availability and demand.

Applications

The resulting IKP Kehati dataset can be used for:

  • Environmental Carrying Capacity (DDLH) analysis
  • Biodiversity carrying capacity assessment
  • Ecosystem service evaluation
  • Watershed management
  • Environmental planning
  • Conservation prioritization
  • Sustainable development assessment
  • D3TLH analysis workflows

Notes

  • All input layers should use the same CRS.
  • Water availability calculations depend on the quality of PYA and WS datasets.
  • Pollution Index values significantly influence usable water availability.
  • Population and land cover data should represent the same analysis year.
  • The quality of the index depends on the completeness and accuracy of all input datasets.
